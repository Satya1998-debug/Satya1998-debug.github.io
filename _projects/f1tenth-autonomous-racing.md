A faculty project in the [School for Talents](https://www.uni-stuttgart.de/en/study/school-for-talents/) programme at the University of Stuttgart (February – June 2024).

The team assembled and programmed an autonomous RC vehicle built to the specifications of the [F1TENTH](https://f1tenth.org/) competition series, a 1:10-scale race car that has to drive a track on its own using onboard sensors and compute, and then raced it against the other participants.
