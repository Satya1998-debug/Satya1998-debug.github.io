Student assistant work at the Institute of Space Systems (IRS), University of Stuttgart (November 2022 – October 2023).

Destiny+ is a Japanese asteroid mission. The piece I built is a Rice encoder in C for the flight software. Rice coding is a lossless compressor that suits the short, low-entropy packets a spacecraft sends home. The encoder is the compression step in the link between the satellite and the ground station.

Related public code: [lossless-data-compression](https://github.com/Satya1998-debug/lossless-data-compression).

Skills: C, data encoding, communication protocols, flight software.
