[Ferienakademie 2025](https://ferienakademie.de/en/home-2/) in Sarntal, Italy (September 2025), run by the Technical University of Munich, FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg and the University of Stuttgart.

I worked in a team of eight on a drone-based real-time monitoring system for Linova Software GmbH. The two weeks also included a Siemens Healthineers course at the High Energy Photon Center.

I wrote up the whole experience in the blog post [Ferienakademie 2025](/blog/2025/Ferienakademie/).
