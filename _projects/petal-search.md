Senior software engineer at Huawei Technologies, India (July 2020 – August 2022).

Petal Search needed structured answers, not only a list of links. I worked on the path from third-party pages to that interface:

- Crawlers and scrapers that ingest pages into the pipeline
- Knowledge-graph features for the search GUI
- REST APIs in Python (Django, Flask, FastAPI) in front of the graph
- Query-identifier and pattern-generator tools that map a search to an intention

The answers showed up as **Unibox** cards right in the results: credit cards, gold rates, exchange rates, cricket scores, income tax, cost of living, express delivery tracking and horoscopes. Each one was fed by its own crawl-and-scrape console application.

The services sat on Elasticsearch, Apache Kafka, and Redis. Prometheus was the place we looked when a flow got slow. Across eight product flows and one cross-platform project, that was more than 28,000 lines.

Skills: Python, Django, Flask, FastAPI, Elasticsearch, Kafka, Redis, Prometheus.
